# SHIELD-AI Case Studies

Sanitised field-validation records produced under Milestone 3 (Field
validation). Each case study exercises the assessment guide
(`assessment-guide/shield-ai-assessment-guide.md`) against a real deployment,
records the resulting scores and findings, and feeds lessons back into the
framework.

## Sanitisation policy

- No client or organisation names; deployments are described by role
  (billing assistant, devops agent) and sector where relevant.
- No credentials, endpoints, or internal identifiers beyond what the repo
  itself publishes.
- Findings are written so they can be shared with prospects without leaking
  the assessed party's posture.
- Raw assessment worksheets stay out of the repo; only the sanitised record
  is published.
