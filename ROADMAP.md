## 7-Day Action Plan

### Days 1-2: Outreach
- Identify 5-10 warm contacts deploying AI agents
- Send personalised note with business deck + offer of 30-min exploratory call

### Days 2-3: Build Lite toolkit
- Structured spreadsheet: 36 controls mapped to scoring columns
- Formulas compute pillar maturity (weakest link) and overall maturity
- Consultant can walk through with a client in ~2 hours

### Days 4-5: First discovery call
- Present SHIELD-AI(TM) Lite
- Convert to paid engagement

### Days 6-7: Start Lite assessment
- Use the assessment guide as the engagement script
- Test every control question and artifact request against a real environment

## Risks to Manage

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **First client discount trap** | Charge full Lite price ($25-45K). If needed, reduce scope at same rate -- never discount the rate. |
| **Builder-seller tension** | Every hour building is an hour not selling. Framework is complete enough. Ship v1.2 after v1.0 revenue. |
| **Over-qualifying leads** | First engagement target is not "perfect client" -- it's "a client who will let us test the guide against real agents." |
| **Self-serve delivering wrong leads** | Scoring must include a qualification gate: if any pillar below L2, route to human conversation. |

## Key Decisions from the Hats Analysis

### What stays unchanged
- Framework v1.1 -- don't touch until engagement 1 feedback
- Assessment guide -- will be tested and refined in first engagement
- Three slide decks -- use them in outreach
- JSON schema + Rego stubs -- good enough for now

### What changes
- Active sales outreach begins now
- Lite self-service toolkit gets built this week

### What was killed
- **SaaS compliance dashboard** (6-12 month detour that delays the essential question: can we sell this?)
- **NZ market expansion** (premature without AU case studies)

### What was deferred
- Rego implementation logic (do per-client after seeing real environments)
- Public publish (do after first case study makes content stronger)
- Partner programme (Year 2)
- Guardrails mapping (positioning paper only until legislation passes)
- State of AI Security report (blog series instead -- lower effort, same positioning)
