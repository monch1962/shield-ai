The Two-Deck Strategy

📊 Deck 1: Business Decision Makers

Audience: CISO, CRO, COO, CIO, GC, Board

Goal: Secure budget and executive sponsorship

Tone: Urgency, regulatory risk, ROI, competitive necessity

Key slides: Why Now (gut punch), 2x2 quadrant positioning, business case table, service tiers with pricing

Duration: 20 minutes + Q&A

💻 Deck 2: Technical SME Deep Dive

Audience: AI engineers, security architects, AI leads, platform engineers, compliance specialists

Goal: Drive technical implementation decisions

Tone: Depth, architecture, decision trees, implementable controls

Key slides: Reference architecture (slide 3), alternatives comparison, per-pillar decision trees, deployment topology guidance

Duration: 45-60 minutes (can be split across sessions)

How to Present Each Deck

📊 Presenting Deck 1 (Business)

  • Start with the "Why Now" story — the gut punch, not the framework
  • Use the 2x2 quadrant to show positioning in 5 seconds
  • Let the business case table speak for itself — stop and let them read
  • Mention blind peer references: "major bank", "government agency"
  • Leave the board briefing slide as a leave-behind (don't present it)
  • After the meeting: Hand them Deck 2 and say "here's how your team will evaluate this."

💻 Presenting Deck 2 (Technical)

  • Start at the reference architecture (slide 3) — this is the anchor
  • Skip slides that aren't relevant to the audience (e.g., skip MCP if they don't use it)
  • Use the alternatives comparison slide to handle "we already use NIST+OWASP" objection
  • For each pillar, present the decision tree first, then the controls
  • If audience is time-pressed: pillars 1-2-5 (MS, IAM, MIR) cover the highest-impact controls
  • End with the "Try It Today" slide to drive action
